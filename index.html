📖 HOW TO PLAY
🃏
FLIP CARDS
Click any card to reveal the symbol underneath.
🔗
FIND PAIRS
Find two matching symbols to lock them in. They glow and stay revealed.
🔥
COMBO STREAKS
Match pairs in a row to build a combo multiplier. Higher combo = more points!
❤️
LIVES
You start with 3 lives. Each wrong match costs a life. Lose all 3 — game over!
💡
HINTS
3 hints per game. Use [H] or the Hint button to briefly reveal a matching pair.
👁
PEEK
Once per game, briefly see all cards (2 seconds). Use it wisely!
⏱
TIMED MODE
Race against a 60-second countdown. Match everything before time runs out!
